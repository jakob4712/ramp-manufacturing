Our story

Metal people, since 1994

Ramp Manufacturing Co. started as a two-bay warehouse with one saw and a phone. Three decades later we're a full-line supply house — but we still answer that phone the same way.

Who we are

A supply partner, not just a vendor

Fabricators don't lose jobs on the metal — they lose them on lead time. We built Ramp around a simple promise: keep deep inventory, quote fast, cut it right, and put it on the dock when we said we would.

That means real people who know the difference between 304 and 316, buyers who work mill-direct every day, and a processing floor that treats a five-piece cut list with the same care as a five-ton release.

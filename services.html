In-house processing

Ready-to-use, not raw

Our processing floor turns stock into finished parts — cut, formed, deburred and kitted to your print. One vendor, one PO, one delivery.

Precision Cutting

Automatic cold saw, band saw and laser cutting for bar, plate and tube — cut-to-length held to ±0.005".

Shearing

CNC shear for sheet and plate up to 3/4" — clean, square edges with tight repeatability across a run.

Forming & Bending

Press brake forming, rolling and bending for brackets, enclosures and structural components.

Deburring & Finishing

Edge break, deburr and surface finishing so parts arrive ready for weld, machine or paint.

Kitting & Bundling

Parts pulled, labeled and bundled by job or workstation to cut your handling time on the floor.

JIT Delivery

Company trucks and vetted carriers deliver on your schedule — next-day across the region.
